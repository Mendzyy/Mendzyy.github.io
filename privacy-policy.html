Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how information is handled by the Android application that links to this page from its Google Play listing.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android application published by Chirag Mendapara that links to this page. The app is intended to process only the information required to provide its features, maintain app reliability, and improve the user experience.

App-specific review required: Before using this URL in Google Play Console, confirm that the data categories below match the exact app implementation and Google Play Data safety form.

2. Data We Collect

Depending on the features enabled in the app, the following categories of information may be collected or processed:

  • Contact information: such as an email address, only when provided by the user for account, support, or communication purposes.
  • Account or profile information: such as display name or authentication identifiers, when the app includes login functionality.
  • App activity and usage information: such as app interactions, diagnostics, crash reports, and performance data used to improve stability.
  • Device and technical information: such as device model, operating system version, app version, language settings, and approximate technical identifiers required for app functionality or security.
  • User-provided content: information entered into the app by the user, if the app provides features for saving or syncing user content.

The app does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information unless this is clearly required for a specific app feature and disclosed in the app experience.

3. How We Use Data

Information may be used to:

  • provide and maintain app features;
  • authenticate users and protect accounts, where login is available;
  • sync data across devices, where cloud sync is available;
  • respond to support requests;
  • monitor app performance, diagnose errors, and improve reliability;
  • comply with legal, security, and platform requirements.

4. Sharing of Information

Information is not sold. Information may be shared only when necessary with service providers that help operate the app, such as hosting, authentication, analytics, crash reporting, database, notification, or payment providers, depending on the app features in use.

Information may also be disclosed if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or to investigate misuse of the app.

5. Third-Party Services

The app may use third-party services such as Google Play services, Firebase, analytics, crash reporting, payment, or cloud hosting providers. These providers may process data according to their own privacy policies and security practices.

6. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the app, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, maintain security, or support legitimate business purposes.

Users may request access, correction, or deletion of their personal information by contacting the developer at the email address below. Some information may need to be retained where required by law, security, fraud prevention, or backup obligations.

8. Children’s Privacy

The app is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children unless the app is specifically designed for children and complies with applicable child privacy laws and Google Play Families requirements. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact the developer so appropriate action can be taken.

9. International Processing

Information may be processed in countries other than the user’s country of residence, depending on the service providers used by the app. Appropriate safeguards are used where required by applicable law.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
